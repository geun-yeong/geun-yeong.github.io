I had a great opportunity to present our paper, **“A Cheating Attack on a Whitelist-based Anti-Ransomware Solution and its Countermeasure,”** at the IEEE International Conference on Consumer Electronics (ICCE) held in Las Vegas.

It was my first time visiting the United States and also my first time giving a presentation in English, so I was quite nervous. Although I was not able to answer the questions during the Q&A session as well as I had hoped, it was still a valuable and memorable experience.

The main contribution of this paper is to propose a more robust whitelist-based ransomware detection and defense solution by detecting and preventing DLL injection attacks, which had been a persistent weakness of our previously developed approach.
