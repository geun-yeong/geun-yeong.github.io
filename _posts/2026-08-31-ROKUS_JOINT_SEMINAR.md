I had the opportunity to present my research on “Development of Off-Chain Data Forensic System Against Cryptocurrency Transaction Obfuscation” at the 2026 ROK-U.S. Joint Seminar for Development of Digital Asset Transaction Tracking Techology to Prevent Malicious Financial Activities in the Digital Asset Market.

It was a valuable experience to share it and exchange ideas with experts from both Korea and the United States.

I hope this collaboration will contribute to advancing technologies for tracking and investigating illicit cryptocurrency transactions.
